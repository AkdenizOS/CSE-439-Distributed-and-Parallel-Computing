## Goals
- Explain sorting networks and parallel bubble/odd-even variants.

## Key concepts
- What makes sorting hard to parallelize: the output is a global ordering.
- **Sorting networks**: compare-exchange, depth as parallel time.
- **Bitonic sort**: bitonic sequences, `Θ(log²n)` depth; mapping onto a hypercube.
- Odd-even transposition sort; parallel shellsort.
